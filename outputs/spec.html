2. Course Educational Objectives (CEOs)
  1. To analyze the asymptotic performance of classical and modern computer algorithms.
  2. To demonstrate proficiency in applying divide-and-conquer, greedy, and dynamic programming paradigms.
  3. To synthesize graph-theoretic models for network flow and traversal optimization.
  4. To understand complexity hierarchies, tractability, and polynomial-time reductions for NP-Complete problems.
3. Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs / COs)
CO # Outcome Statement Bloom Level Attainment
CO1 Explain asymptotic complexity notations, recurrence relations, and algorithmic efficiency models across best, average, and worst-case bounds. L2 (Understand) 75%
CO2 Apply divide-and-conquer, greedy selection, and dynamic programming techniques to solve constrained algorithmic optimization problems. L3 (Apply) 70%
CO3 Analyze minimum spanning tree, shortest path, and maximum network flow algorithms on sparse and dense graph topologies. L4 (Analyze) 70%
CO4 Differentiate polynomial vs non-deterministic polynomial problem classes (P, NP, NP-Complete) using formal reduction proofs. L4 (Analyze) 65%
CO5 Evaluate randomized and approximation heuristics for computationally intractable NP-hard combinatorial bottlenecks. L5 (Evaluate) 60%
CO6 Design branch-and-bound and state-space search architectures for high-dimensional computational decision systems. L6 (Create) 65%

5. Modular Syllabus Outline
UNIT I: INTRODUCTION & ASYMPTOTIC ANALYSIS 12 Contact Hours
Order Notation (O, Omega, Theta), Master Theorem, Substitution Method, Recursion Tree, Recurrences, Amortized Analysis (Accounting & Potential Methods).
Mapped CO: CO1 | Pedagogy: Chalk & Talk, Interactive Slides, Proof Workshop
UNIT II: DIVIDE & CONQUER AND GREEDY PARADIGMS 12 Contact Hours
Strassen Matrix Multiplication, Convex Hull, Greedy Choice Property, Optimal Substructure, Fractional Knapsack, Huffman Coding, Job Sequencing with Deadlines.
Mapped CO: CO2 | Pedagogy: Chalk & Talk, Algorithm Visualization Demo, Peer Code Walkthrough
UNIT III: DYNAMIC PROGRAMMING & GRAPH ALGORITHMS 12 Contact Hours
Matrix Chain Multiplication, Longest Common Subsequence (LCS), 0/1 Knapsack, Bellman-Ford, Floyd-Warshall, Johnson Algorithm, Maximum Bipartite Matching, Ford-Fulkerson.
Mapped COs: CO2, CO3 | Pedagogy: Visual Simulation, Problem-Based Learning Group Activity
UNIT IV: TRACTABILITY & NP-COMPLETENESS 12 Contact Hours
Deterministic vs Non-deterministic Turing Machines, Polynomial Reductions, Cook-Levin Theorem, Satisfiability (3-SAT), Clique, Vertex Cover, Hamiltonian Cycle, TSP.
Mapped CO: CO4 | Pedagogy: Chalk & Talk, Case Studies in Intractability, Socratic Seminar
UNIT V: APPROXIMATION, RANDOMIZED & BRANCH-AND-BOUND 12 Contact Hours
Approximation Ratio, Vertex Cover 2-Approximation, Metric TSP, Random Treaps, Quickselect Expected Bounds, Branch and Bound LC-Search, 15-Puzzle Problem, Assignment Problem.
Mapped COs: CO5, CO6 | Pedagogy: Interactive Benchmark Coding, Research Paper Seminar
